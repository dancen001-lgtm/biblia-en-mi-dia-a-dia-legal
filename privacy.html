Privacy

This is written to be understood, not to cover anyone's back.

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · En español

The important part first: this app collects nothing.

There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours. What you highlight, what you write down, where you left off reading and how you set the app up stays inside your phone. Uninstall the app and it goes with it.

No ads and no trackers. The app is a gift: nothing about your behaviour is measured, nothing is sold to anyone, and there is never anything to pay.

No account, no name

We never ask for your email, your name or your phone number. We don't know who you are or how often you opened the app — and that isn't a promise, it's that there is nowhere to send it. The app never writes to a server of ours, because we don't have one.

The microphone

You can work the app by speaking — "Matthew 5, 14 to 23" — and that needs the microphone. How it works matters:

  • The microphone is not always on. It opens when you open it, and closes by itself.
  • What you say is recognised inside your phone, by a program that lives in the app. No internet needed.
  • The audio is never stored and never sent anywhere. It becomes a command and is thrown away.

If you never use voice control, the app doesn't even ask for the permission.

When it uses the internet

The whole Spanish Bible, the voice that reads it and the speech recognition all come inside the installer: the app works completely offline from the first moment.

The internet is used only if you ask for something optional that was left out for size — today, the English language. That is downloaded from third-party servers:

FromWhat it downloads
huggingface.coVoices that read aloud
github.comBackup copy of those same voices
alphacephei.comThe speech recognition

As with any download, those servers see the internet address (IP) the request comes from. We send them nothing about you, and we learn nothing either: the download goes straight from the server to your phone.

When something goes wrong

The app writes down the errors it runs into, and keeps them on your phone. They are not sent anywhere. They are there so that you can show them yourself if you ask for help, and you can delete them whenever you want.

Google Play

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google collects things of its own — crash reports, for instance — under its own privacy policy. That happens with every app in the store and is not in our hands. We receive none of it with your name on it.

Deleting everything

There is no account to delete. To erase what is stored on the phone you have two ways, and you control both:

  • Inside the app, under Settings, each thing can be deleted separately.
  • Uninstall the app, which erases all of it at once.

The Bible texts

The versions included are public domain or freely licensed, and they are credited inside the app. No translation still under copyright was used: something being free doesn't take away anyone's rights.

Children

The app is suitable for any age and collects data from nobody, children included.

Changes

If this changes, the date above changes. If the change is a big one, you are told inside the app.
